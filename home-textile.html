🇧🇩 Why Source Home Textiles from Bangladesh?
🛡
Duty-Free EU AccessEverything But Arms (EBA) scheme grants quota- and duty-free access for Bangladeshi home textiles to EU markets — a major cost advantage vs. China, Pakistan, and India.
🏭
Established Export BaseBangladesh exports home textiles to 47+ countries including the USA, UK, Germany, France, Canada, Japan, and Australia. Major buyers include IKEA, Walmart, ASDA, and Carrefour.
🧵
Specialist Terry CapacityBangladesh has dedicated terry towel manufacturers with state-of-the-art facilities — thread counts from 180–600 TC for bedding and 400–800 GSM for terry products.
💰
Competitive PricingLower labour costs combined with backward integration into yarn and fabric means factory-direct pricing with no unnecessary markups.

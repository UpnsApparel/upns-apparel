Quality Control

You should not have to inspect your own shipment.

Every problem in a bulk order was a missed decision point earlier. Our QC process catches them before they become problems.

1
Fabric inspection before cutting

Weight, colour, shrinkage, and defects checked before a panel is cut. Wrong fabric means a stopped order.

2
Inline QC during sewing

Stitching count, seam strength, label placement checked at the line. Issues caught at 100 pieces, not 10,000.

3
Measurement audit on finished bulk

Random samples measured against your spec sheet. Size consistency confirmed before packing.

4
Pre-shipment inspection — AQL 2.5

Final inspection against your sealed sample. Pass rate confirmed. Loading photos sent. Nothing ships without your visibility.
